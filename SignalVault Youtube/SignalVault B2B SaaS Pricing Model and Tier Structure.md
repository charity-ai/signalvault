# SignalVault B2B SaaS Pricing Model and Tier Structure

**Prepared for:** Charity  
**Prepared by:** Manus AI  
**Date:** September 5, 2026

## Strategic Position

**SignalVault should not be priced as a YouTube analytics or transcript tool.** Analytics, optimization, and creator-assistance platforms are already anchored to low creator software prices. vidIQ, for example, markets a $39 per month Max plan for advanced creator growth tools, while another channel-analytics provider lists $19, $49, and $149 monthly plans for one channel, three channels, and enterprise analytics. [1] [2] Competing there would force SignalVault into high-volume, low-margin support.

SignalVault is instead a **business knowledge and authority infrastructure product**. It turns an expert-led company’s video archive into a private, cited resource for sales, content, operations, and client delivery. The buyer is paying to make existing intellectual property usable, protect brand consistency, shorten research, surface proof, and create assets that support a high-ticket offer. That value supports a higher B2B price, an activation fee, and a recurring operating model.

> **Positioning statement:** SignalVault turns an expert’s video library into a private, cited authority system that helps the team answer faster, create stronger sales assets, and convert existing content into qualified demand.

## Pricing Principles

The model has four rules. First, charge for the **business outcome**, not minutes of video or generic AI access. Second, use a required activation fee because source connection, archive ingestion, evidence-quality review, and workspace configuration create real work and meaningful customer value. Third, use channels, private workspaces, seats, source capacity, and Intelligence Actions as the metered value boundaries. Fourth, preserve a clean upgrade path from individual expert to multi-client agency.

An **Intelligence Action** is a transparent usage unit for high-compute work. One cited channel answer or saved research brief uses one action. A source-grounded asset, such as a sales playbook, email sequence, video brief, or objection library, uses five actions. A full public Reference report uses ten actions. Ordinary browsing, searching, and viewing already-indexed sources do not use actions. This controls variable artificial-intelligence costs without presenting the customer with confusing token math.

### 4. Enterprise Knowledge Vault: from $6,000 per month

Enterprise is a sales-led plan for a multi-brand content business, education company, large agency, or organization with proprietary knowledge, several experts, complex access controls, or procurement requirements. Use annual commitments only.

Enterprise begins at five private workspaces and five owner-authorized channels. Price increases according to the number of segregated workspaces, archive volume, integration complexity, security review, data residency or retention requirements, service-level expectations, and implementation labor. A reasonable floor is **$6,000 per month on an annual agreement**, plus **$15,000 implementation**. Do not quote a fixed “unlimited” tier.

The Founding Authority Vault is discounted from the standard Authority Vault rate, but it is not cheap. This creates cash flow and enables proof without training the market to expect bargain pricing.

## Sales Boundaries and Contract Terms

Every paid plan should include a monthly subscription term after activation, 30 days’ notice before cancellation, and data export or deletion controls. Annual plans should be paid in advance. Custom integrations, historical archive cleanup, data migration, and new workflow design are separate implementation work.

Do not sell “unlimited” channels, source hours, retrieval, or artificial intelligence generation. Use transparent plan capacity and paid expansion units. Do not offer a money-back guarantee after a full archive has been indexed. Instead, offer a paid Authority Sprint or a seven-day workspace preview with a capped source set before the buyer authorizes a full ingestion.

For privacy and data reliability, keep public Reference data separate from owner-authorized Vault sources. YouTube’s official APIs support channel-owner analytics reporting and targeted queries, while caption download requires authorization to edit the associated video. [3] [4] The contract and interface should make this source distinction explicit.

## Immediate Commercial Actions

The first offer to sell is the **Founding Authority Vault**. Use the current SignalVault prototype as the visual sales environment, but describe it accurately as a guided beta until ingestion, cited retrieval, and source controls are live. Create a short application that requires a channel URL, current offer, approximate video count, monthly content output, sales-team structure, and the highest-value question the buyer wishes their archive could answer.

Then build one demonstrably successful internal implementation on Charity’s own channel. The launch asset is not a feature list. It is a short case study showing the number of videos indexed, the number of proof points surfaced, the source-backed asset created, the time saved, and the revenue action that followed.
